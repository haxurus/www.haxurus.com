04 / SCOPE

How pricing works

The displayed prices are entry points, not fixed packages. A small edit and a production system are different jobs even when they use the same technology.

Good fit for a quick quote

Small websites, targeted fixes, scripts, bot features, server setup, migrations, troubleshooting and clearly bounded integrations can usually be scoped directly from a concise brief.

Needs a custom estimate

Large websites, multi-service applications, infrastructure redesigns, complex networks, business environments and long-term maintenance are estimated after the requirements and existing environment are clear.

05 / FAQ

Before you commission

A few practical details that help set expectations before a project starts.

Can you work on an existing project instead of starting from scratch?

Yes. I can work on existing websites, codebases, servers, networks and community tooling when the current state is accessible and the requested change can be scoped safely.

Are the displayed prices final?

No. They are starting prices for small and clearly defined work. You receive the actual quote before I begin, based on the agreed scope and technical complexity.

Can I request something that is not listed as a service?

Yes. If it falls within my technical stack, send the idea anyway. I will tell you whether it is something I can reasonably take on.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Yes. Maintenance can cover websites, servers, deployments, backups, updates, monitoring and recurring technical support depending on the project.

Do you provide source code or documentation?

When the commission includes custom code, the expected handoff is agreed in the scope. Relevant deployment or operating notes can also be included when needed.

Can you work with private or company infrastructure?

Yes, when access, authorization and scope are clear. I only work within systems and environments you are authorized to provide access to.
