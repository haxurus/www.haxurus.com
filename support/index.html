FAQ

Before you join

A few useful answers about memberships and supporter perks.

Is Patreon support recurring?

Yes. Patreon memberships are recurring subscriptions billed according to Patreon's current billing rules. You can manage, change or cancel your membership from your Patreon account.

Do I need to become a supporter to follow your projects?

No. Public projects and normal community activity remain available without a paid membership. Supporting is optional and mainly gives extra access, previews and selected perks.

What does the money help pay for?

Support helps cover recurring costs such as hosting, domains, online services, software, project assets and other expenses connected to websites, VRChat worlds, community tools and future experiments.

When do Discord and VRChat perks become available?

After Patreon confirms an active membership. Some perks may require you to connect or identify the relevant Discord or VRChat account so I can apply the correct role or access manually.

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Yes. You can change your tier from Patreon whenever you want. Patreon will show how the change affects your billing before you confirm it.

Can supporter perks change over time?

Yes, because the projects themselves evolve. If a perk becomes obsolete or technically impossible to maintain, I may adjust it or replace it with something comparable and communicate the change.

Does supporting guarantee that a specific project will be completed?

No. Support helps me dedicate more resources to my work, but experimental projects can change, pause or be cancelled if they stop being viable. I will always try to be transparent about meaningful changes.
